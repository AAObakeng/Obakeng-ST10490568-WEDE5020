Welcome to The Xperiment Digital Archive, an Independent digital archive documenting Ken Carson's Xperiment era.

This website explores the album's rollout, promotional campaign, visual identity, media coverage and importance moments surrounding the project.

ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

THE XPERIMENT ERA

Ken didn't drop xperiment. He performed it into existence.

From May to July 2026, the rollout happened on stage. Ken debuted "Out Of My Body", "Where Do I Start", and "Cover My Ears" live before they hit streaming.

The album cover — Ken with a USB port in his neck — told you everything: this was music as experiment, and Ken was the test subject.

On camera and off, he was hands-on. Creative directing his photos, modeling his own fits, and building the red-lit, glitchy world of xperiment piece by piece.

"I think my style evolves every single day."

This archive is that evolution. Every photo, every moment, every fit from the era that solidified Ken as a niche superstar.
