ABOUT THE PROJECT

The Xperiment Digital Archive is an independent digital archive created to document Ken Carson's xperiment era.

The archive brings together information about the project, its promotion, visual identity, media coverage and important events.



ABOUT XPERIMENT

xperiment is the fifth studio album by Ken Carson. The album was released on July 3, 2026 through Opium and Interscope Records. is the main project documented by this archive. This section provides visitors with background information about the album and its place within Ken Carson's musical career. If Playboi Carti gave you the blueprint, Ken Carson is in the lab blowing it up. Dropped in 2024, XPERIMENT is 16 tracks of pure controlled chaos. 23 minutes, no skips, no filler.

The project contains 22 tracks and has an overall runtime of 60 minutes and 41 seconds.

The visual concept of the project focuses on a futuristic laboratory and science-fiction aesthetic, with a USB drive connected to Ken Carson's neck as a central visual element.
